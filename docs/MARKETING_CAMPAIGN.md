# SolarSearch — Go-to-Market & Marketing Campaign

**Objective:** turn SolarSearch from a built platform into a competitive, active,
revenue-generating solar marketplace — starting at the **1 Aug 2026 go-live**.

> Assumptions to confirm (flagged so they're easy to change): **NSW-first**
> geography (Ausgrid / Endeavour / Essential footprint); demand-led launch with
> a parallel supply-readiness track; monthly paid budget presented as tiers, not
> a fixed number. Adjust these three and the plan flexes.

---

## 1. The wedge (why we win)

Every volume competitor (Sunboost, Arise, 1Komma5, the lead-resellers) sells on
**price and speed**. None can credibly sell on **proof**. SolarSearch can, because
compliance and reputation are built into the product:

- Every install is captured, AI-verified, and **compliance-packed** (Solarsafe).
- Installers are **vetted** and accredited before they touch a job.
- The customer **always chooses** their quote — no bait-and-switch, no pressure.

**One-line positioning:**
> *"The only place to get solar where every installer is vetted and every install
> is proven compliant — you choose the quote, we guarantee the standard."*

This reframes the category from *"cheapest panels"* to *"safe, audit-proof solar
you can trust."* It is defensible, on-trend (post-cowboy-installer sentiment,
tightening CER/NSW rules from mid-2026), and it justifies a **quality premium**
instead of a race to the bottom.

## 2. The two sides (and the sequencing trap)

A marketplace dies if it drives one side without the other:

| Side | Who | What they need to see |
|---|---|---|
| **Demand** | Homeowners (+ small commercial) wanting solar/battery | Trust, a fast free assessment, real comparable quotes |
| **Supply** | Vetted **installers**, gig **sales technicians**, **retailers**, edge-protection & cleaning contractors | A steady flow of qualified, pre-assessed jobs |

**Sequencing rule for launch:** supply must be *ready* before demand is *scaled*.
The platform already has onboarding built; the job is to **provision real
accounts** (GO_LIVE_PLAN item 1) so day-1 leads have fulfilment. Then open the
demand tap. Over-driving homeowner ads with no installers = angry leads and a
dead reputation — the one thing our whole wedge can't afford.

**Launch ratio:** ~70% budget/effort to **demand generation**, ~30% to
**supply recruitment**, rebalanced weekly against the *lead-to-installer* ratio
(target: no postcode gets homeowner ads until it has ≥2 active installers +
1 sales tech).

## 3. Target segments & messaging

### Demand — homeowners (primary revenue driver)
Derived from the six existing×wants scenarios (see CUSTOMER_SCENARIOS.md), but for
marketing collapse to three intents:

| Segment | Hook | Proof point |
|---|---|---|
| **New solar** (has nothing) | "Free roof assessment. Vetted installers. You pick the quote." | Compliance pack + accreditation |
| **Battery add** (has solar) | "Add a battery the right way — checked for your existing inverter first." | Nameplate-capture assessment = honest quote, no surprises |
| **Expansion / hybrid** (has solar±battery) | "Grow your system without guesswork." | Increment-only rebate maths = honest savings |

Emotional spine across all three: **trust and no-regret**. Rational spine:
**federal Cheaper Home Batteries rebate + STCs**, quantified honestly.

### Supply — the fulfilment side
| Role | Pitch | Channel |
|---|---|---|
| **Installers** | "Pre-assessed, pre-photographed jobs. Buy a seat, quote, win. No lead-chasing." | Direct outreach, installer FB/WhatsApp groups, SEIA/industry lists |
| **Sales technicians** (gig) | "Turn up, capture 12 photos, get paid. No selling." | Gig-work channels, local classifieds, referral |
| **Retailers** | "Place jobs, keep your brand, we run compliant fulfilment." | 1:1 BD (this is the B2B channel the standalone site tried to serve) |

## 4. Channel plan

### Demand generation
1. **Google Search (highest intent, launch day 1)** — capture existing demand.
   Keywords: *"solar quotes NSW", "home battery rebate NSW", "add battery to
   solar", "solar installer [suburb]"*. Landing page: **`quote.html`** (instant
   roof-scan estimate) → book assessment. This is the money channel — start here.
2. **Meta (Facebook/Instagram) — demand creation + trust.** Video/creative on the
   "cowboy installer" fear → "vetted + compliance-proven" relief. Geo-fenced to
   launch postcodes with live supply. Retarget `quote.html` visitors who didn't book.
3. **SEO / content (compounding, start now).** Rank for *"Cheaper Home Batteries
   rebate NSW", "is my solar installer accredited", "solar rebate 2026"*. The
   compliance angle is genuinely differentiated content nobody else writes.
4. **Referral loop.** Post-install, the compliance pack + tech badge is a
   share-worthy artifact ("here's proof my install is compliant") → referral
   incentive. Turns fulfilled jobs into demand.
5. **Comparison/aggregator presence** (SolarQuotes-style directories) — list as the
   "compliance-first" option.

### Supply recruitment
- **Installer BD blitz** (weeks -2 → +4): direct outreach in launch postcodes,
  "first N installers get reduced seat fee." Seed liquidity.
- **Sales-tech recruitment**: gig channels + a simple `apply.html` funnel; the
  toolkit (TECH_TOOLKIT.md) is the onboarding asset.
- **Retailer partnerships**: 1:1, higher-touch, longer cycle — start conversations
  now, they convert over the quarter.

## 5. The funnel (map spend to the real pipeline)

```
Ad / SEO  →  quote.html (instant estimate)  →  book free assessment (capture_lead)
          →  sales-tech / consultant visit  →  quote (installers compete)
          →  choose.html (customer picks)   →  sign.html  →  install (compliant)
```

Instrument each hop so spend is judged on **cost per booked assessment** and
**cost per signed job**, not clicks. The assessment booking is the true
"activation" event — optimise ad channels toward it.

**Conversion levers already in the product** (use them in creative):
- *Instant roof-scan estimate* (quote.html) — low-friction entry.
- *Free assessment* — removes cost objection.
- *Customer always chooses* — removes pressure objection.
- *Compliance pack + vetted installers* — removes trust objection.

## 6. Budget scenarios (paid media/month, NSW launch)

Presented as tiers — pick per appetite; all assume Google-first, then Meta.

| Tier | Monthly paid | Shape | Realistic outcome* |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Seed** | $3–5k | Google Search only, 3–5 launch postcodes | Prove CPL + funnel; ~tens of assessments/mo |
| **Launch** | $8–15k | Google + Meta, 10–15 postcodes, retargeting | Fill day-1 supply; steady signed jobs |
| **Scale** | $25k+ | Add creative testing, SEO investment, expand postcodes | Category share, compounding pipeline |

\* *Ranges depend on real CPL from the Seed phase — always calibrate up from
measured cost-per-assessment, never from a projected number.*

Guardrail: **never spend into a postcode without live supply.** Budget follows
liquidity.

## 8. KPIs (the dashboard to watch)

- **North star:** signed jobs / month, and **CAC vs gross margin per job**.
- Funnel: cost per booked assessment → assessment→quote rate → quote→sign rate.
- Supply health: active installers & sales techs per postcode; lead-to-installer ratio.
- Quality (the moat): first-time compliance-pass rate, customer trust/NPS,
  referral rate. Protect these — they *are* the brand.

## 9. What makes this competitive (summary)

- A **trust position no competitor can copy** without rebuilding their whole model.
- Spend **tied to fulfilment liquidity**, so growth never outruns quality.
- A funnel already instrumented from **instant estimate → compliant install**.
- Revenue from **multiple streams** (installer seats + winner fees, edge-protection,
  cleaning, subcontract commission, STC) — the campaign fills the top; the platform
  monetises the whole chain.
